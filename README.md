# 🏡 HomeService Management System

This repository contains the codebase for the **HomeService Management System**, a platform designed to connect users with professionals who offer various home services such as cleaning, plumbing, electrical work, and more. 
The platform will allow users to browse services, book appointments, and manage ongoing requests, while service providers can manage their schedules, track jobs, and get customer feedback.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

The **HomeService Management System** aims to provide a seamless experience for customers to find reliable home service providers and book services quickly. 
Service providers will also have a dedicated space to manage their jobs, interact with clients, and monitor their business performance.

### 🛠️ Planned Features
1. **User Roles & Authentication**:
   - Role-based access for customers and service providers.
   - Secure authentication with email verification and role-specific dashboards.

2. **Service Listings & Categories**:
   - Browse and search for services across various categories (e.g., cleaning, plumbing, electrical repairs).
   - View service details, pricing, and provider ratings.

3. **Booking & Scheduling**:
   - Real-time booking system allowing customers to select available time slots.
   - Service providers can manage availability and view upcoming appointments.

4. **Job Management**:
   - Service providers can track job status (pending, in-progress, completed).
   - Customers can manage their bookings (reschedule or cancel appointments).

5. **Payments & Invoices**:
   - Integrated payment gateway for secure transactions.
   - Generate invoices and receipts for completed jobs.

6. **Reviews & Ratings**:
   - Customers can rate and review service providers after job completion.
   - Providers can respond to reviews and maintain a reputation score.

7. **Notifications & Alerts**:
   - SMS/Email notifications for job confirmations, updates, and reminders.
   - Alerts for both customers and providers regarding upcoming appointments.

8. **Admin Dashboard (Planned Expansion)**:
   - A comprehensive admin dashboard for managing users, services, and handling disputes.

## 🏗️ Tech Stack (Planned)
The following technologies will be used for the development of the **HomeService Management System**:

- **Backend**:
  - **C#**, **ASP.NET Core** for backend development and APIs.
  - **Entity Framework Core** for database management.
  - **MySQL**  as the relational database.

- **Frontend**:
  - **React.js**  for building a responsive user interface.
  - **Tailwind CSS** for modern, responsive styling.

- **Authentication & Authorization**:
  - **JWT (JSON Web Tokens)** for secure user authentication and role-based access control.

- **Payments Integration(Future Implementation**:
  -  API for handling payments.

- **Notifications**:
  - **Twilio** for SMS notifications, and email service providers for email alerts.

- **Infrastructure**:
  - **Docker** for containerization and deployment.
  - **Azure**  for cloud hosting and scaling.

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

1. **Efficiency**: Simplify the process for customers to book services and for providers to manage their business.
2. **Reliability**: Provide a platform that offers trusted service providers with verified reviews and secure transactions.
3. **Scalability**: Build a system that can scale with a growing user base and support multiple service categories.
4. **User-Friendly**: Ensure the platform is intuitive and easy to use for both customers and service providers.
